## Definition of done for this sprint

- `CT_OfficeStyleSheet` reads and writes the complete theme contract, including
  `a:fmtScheme`, while retaining unsupported XML in its original schema slots.
- The Office theme generated by the pinned PowerPoint 16.104 oracle parses,
  serialises, and reparses to a structurally equal value.
- `office_default()` produces the standard Office theme in schema order and a
  theme that PowerPoint accepts.
- The `rdocx_oxml::theme::Theme` adapter consumes the shared theme without
  changing existing Word tint, shade, or rendering behaviour.
- The external corpus-wide M7 gate transfers to F-067 at S16 entry, where the
  corpus harness and pinned source are established. Inline structural coverage
  remains mandatory in S15 and is not represented as corpus evidence.
- The full workspace gate passes with all 28 deterministic hashes unchanged,
  and no crate is published.
